# RouteLogic Velocity – Future State Journey Map

## Initiative
**Strip & Accelerate** – Reclaim operational simplicity by removing legacy workflow friction and making RouteLogic the fastest platform for everyday logistics operations.

## Persona
**Frontline Logistics Coordinator**

**Goal**
Complete shipment planning and updates quickly and accurately while minimizing delays and manual effort.

**Current Friction**
The platform is too complex and slow for everyday tasks, forcing users to switch between spreadsheets, emails, carrier portals, and multiple RouteLogic screens.

## Stage 1 — Unified Planning

**User Action**  
Open RouteLogic and immediately view prioritized shipments in one workspace.

**Internal State**  
Confident and organized.

**Pain Point Addressed**  
Eliminates searching across spreadsheets, emails, and multiple dashboards.

**Action → Benefit**  
View prioritized shipments → Start planning immediately.

---

## Stage 2 — Smart Decision Support

**User Action**  
Review AI-assisted routing suggestions and shipment exceptions.

**Internal State**  
Focused and in control.

**Pain Point Addressed**  
Removes repetitive validation and manual cross-checking.

**Action → Benefit**  
Accept recommendations → Reduce manual planning effort.

---

## Stage 3 — One-Click Execution

**User Action**  
Update shipment details once and automatically synchronize all related workflows.

**Internal State**  
Efficient and productive.

**Pain Point Addressed**  
Eliminates duplicate entry and inconsistent records.

**Action → Benefit**  
Update once → Synchronize every workflow automatically.

---

## Stage 4 — Monitor & Complete

**User Action**  
Track shipment progress and resolve exceptions from a unified dashboard.

**Internal State**  
Confident and proactive.

**Pain Point Addressed**  
Removes manual follow-up through emails and spreadsheets.

**Action → Benefit**  
Resolve exceptions → Deliver shipments faster.

---

# Competitive Advantages

1. One integrated workflow replaces spreadsheets, emails, and carrier portals.
2. AI-assisted planning reduces clicks, manual validation, and shipment delays.
3. Real-time synchronization improves productivity, accuracy, and customer trust.

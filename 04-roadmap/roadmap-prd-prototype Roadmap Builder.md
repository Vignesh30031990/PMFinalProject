# Feature Roadmap, Module 4 · RouteLogic Velocity

**Team:** 2 engineers + 1 designer + 1 CS lead

## Strategic anchors
- **Persona:** Frontline Logistics Coordinator
- **Primary metric:** Increase end-to-end Coordinator Daily Dispatch Workflow completion from 18% to at least 35%
- **Moment of misery:** Compliance and downstream administrative workflows are too slow and complex, forcing coordinators into manual workarounds.
- **Guardrail:** Maintain Live Dispatch Board coordinator adoption at ≥90%

## Roadmap
### NOW, Pilot (4 weeks, 3 accounts)
- **B1 One-Click Compliance Checklist**, Directly attacks the 14.6-min compliance bottleneck and 48% workflow completion point.
- **B2 Smart Daily Report Auto-Fill**, Reduces reporting friction where only 18% complete the end-to-end workflow.
- **B3 Shift Handoff Wizard**, Addresses 11.3-min handoff time and the drop to 31% workflow completion

### NEXT, GA Release (weeks 5-8)
- **B4 Mobile-First Coordinator Dashboard**, Supports the persona, but requires substantial redesign beyond the immediate bottlenecks.
- **B5 Step Progress Indicator**, Low-effort improvement that improves workflow visibility after core friction is addressed.
- **B9 Compliance Audit Trail Export**, Supports compliance needs, but first simplify the coordinator's core compliance workflow.

### LATER, backlog
- **B6 Driver Alert Notifications**, Useful operationally but does not directly address the validated coordinator bottleneck.
- **B7 Contextual AI ETA Display**, Predictive ETA adoption is low, so evidence for prioritizing it is currently weak.
- **B10 In-App Coordinator Training**, Training may help adoption but does not remove the underlying workflow complexity.

### ✂ Cut List
- **B8 Fleet Analytics Manager View**, Manager-focused Sales request that does not address our primary persona's validated friction.

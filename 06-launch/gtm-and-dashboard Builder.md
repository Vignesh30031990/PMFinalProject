# GTM Launch Plan, RouteLogic (B2B)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Feature | One-Click Compliance Checklist — a smart pre-filled workflow that lets coordinators review, correct, validate, and submit compliance information in one flow. |
| Goal | Engagement |
| Launch tier | M, Targeted |

## Goal & Audience
- **Goal:** Engagement, The feature improves an existing daily RouteLogic workflow rather than creating market awareness. The priority is deeper frontline adoption by reducing compliance friction and helping coordinators complete dispatch work inside RouteLogic.
- **Target audience:** Primary: Frontline Logistics Coordinators responsible for daily shipment planning, tracking, compliance, and updates. Secondary: Operations managers overseeing coordinator productivity and workflow completion.

## Launch Tier
- **M, Targeted**, A targeted launch fits an existing B2B workflow enhancement. The feature directly affects coordinators and their managers, but does not require broad market awareness or paid acquisition. Focused customer communication, in-product guidance, and Customer Success enablement are sufficient.

## Channels
1. **Owned: In-app announcement and contextual guidance shown to coordinators entering the compliance workflow.**
2. **Owned: Targeted email to logistics coordinators and operations managers explaining the simplified compliance workflow and expected time savings.**
3. **Owned: Customer Success-led enablement through account communications, short demos, and rollout sessions with pilot customers.**

## Enablement & Assets
Create an in-app walkthrough, one-page quick-start guide, short workflow demo, CS enablement brief, FAQ, and manager communication template. Support receives known validation scenarios and escalation guidance before launch.

## Ownership, Budget & Timeline
- **Ownership & budget:** PM owns launch coordination, adoption metrics, and post-launch decisions. Product/Engineering own release readiness and instrumentation. Design owns in-app guidance and quick-start assets. Customer Success owns customer communications and enablement. Support owns issue triage. Use existing owned channels; no paid-media budget required.
- **Timeline:** Phase 1 — Week 1: validate instrumentation, brief CS/Support, finalize enablement assets, and launch to pilot accounts. Phase 2 — Weeks 2–3: expand to eligible coordinator users with in-app guidance and targeted communications. Phase 3 — Week 4: review workflow completion, adoption, accuracy, feedback, and decide whether to expand, iterate, or pause.

## Success Metrics
- **Metrics:** End-to-end Coordinator Daily Dispatch Workflow completion improves from 18% toward ≥28%.
One-Click Compliance Checklist adoption among eligible coordinators reaches ≥60%.
Live Dispatch Board coordinator adoption remains ≥90%.
- **Bad signal to watch for:** Checklist adoption increases but workflow completion does not improve, compliance accuracy declines, or Live Dispatch Board adoption falls below 90%.
- **Likely post-launch decision:** Expand if workflow completion improves by ≥10 percentage points while guardrails hold; iterate if adoption is strong but completion lift is below target; pause if accuracy declines or core dispatch adoption falls below 90%.
